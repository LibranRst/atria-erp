# Rule: Material Control, Inventory & RAP/RAB Architecture

This rule defines the operational lifecycle and data boundaries for Material Master, RAP execution plans, client-facing RAB, Inventory Stock, and Stock Movements in **Atria ERP**.

## 1. RAP vs. RAB Strict Separation
- **RAP (Rencana Anggaran Pelaksanaan)**:
  - Internal execution baseline detailing material quantities, unit costs, internal labor plans, subcontractor scopes, and vendor costs.
  - Represents the cost floor and margin foundation.
  - Locked upon project budget finalization.
- **RAB (Rencana Anggaran Biaya)**:
  - Client-facing estimate / proposal.
  - May be generated by selecting RAP items, Tukang packages, and Subcon packages.
  - **ISOLATION INVARIANT**: Internal RAP costs, markups, vendor identities, internal margin calculations, and internal notes must NEVER be exposed in client-facing RAB outputs or exports.

---

## 2. Inventory Balance & Stock States
Every material tracked in `material_master` maintains three stock quantities:
- **`current_stock`**: Total physical inventory currently in the warehouse/storage.
- **`reserved_stock`**: Total quantity committed/reserved for active projects.
- **`available_stock`**: Uncommitted inventory available for new reservations (`available = current - reserved`).

---

## 3. Stock Movement Types & Invariants
All physical and logical inventory updates must record a `stock_movement` row and execute within `db.transaction`:
- **`stock_in`**: Physical receipt of purchased materials (Goods Receipt) or opening inventory. Increases `current_stock`.
- **`reserve`**: Allocating available stock to a specific project. Increases `reserved_stock` (does NOT decrease `current_stock` or affect cash).
- **`use` (Consume)**: Physical deployment of reserved material to a project site. Decreases `current_stock` and decreases `reserved_stock`.
- **`return`**: Returning unused material from a project back to warehouse stock. Increases `current_stock`.
- **`transfer`**: Moving stock between warehouse locations.
- **`adjustment` / `waste`**: Reconciling physical audit discrepancies or recorded material breakage. MUST include an explanatory audit note.

---

## 4. Cash Flow vs. Stock Event Decoupling
- Material Reservation (`reserve`) does NOT generate a cash transaction or expense.
- Physical usage (`use`) does NOT create a cash transaction by itself; cash outflow is tracked exclusively via vendor bills (`vendor_bill`) and field payment claims (`project_cost`).
- Goods Receipt (`stock_in`) verifies physical arrival and updates inventory; the corresponding vendor payment is tracked through Finance's invoice approval workflow.

---

## 5. Tukang and Subcon Packages
- **Tukang Work Package**: Internal labor schedule linked directly to `TUKANG` category expenses. Does not trigger stock movements or warehouse inventory.
- **Subcon Package**: Subcontractor scope plan linked directly to `SUBCON` category vendor bills and payments.
