## Implementation

- Strict pure core DTOs and discriminated actions; real IDs, bounded unique rows, finite database-safe amounts/quantities, human dates/notes/reasons. Source analysis must disclose truncation or unsupported mappings, never infer an amount or convert units silently. Supported chat attachment ownership/OCR remains authoritative; no Google Sheets URL fetching.
- Vendor bills reuse existing owned 30-day VENDOR_BILL drafts and shared transactional writer. Draft saving and submission are separate actions. Respect live create permission and project scope, category/package/material constraints, duplicate bill number and source ownership. Explain that submission creates an unpaid bill, not a payment approval; Material purchases are inventory assets and do not instantly consume Material actuals. Retain invoice provenance and line details without fabricating material links for a multi-material invoice.
- Extract transaction-level transfer/count writers from current manual actions and preserve public signatures. Transfer protects reservations and weighted valuation. Stock-count drafts do not change inventory; submission requests existing approval, then approval requires an independent checker, current unchanged stock and adequate reservation coverage. Emergency manual adjustment remains the explicitly protected ordinary form; normal AI stock corrections use stock opname approval. Preserve existing notification and audit behavior.
- Read-only tools discover paginated actual references and prepare immutable owned proposals. Fixed backend read/confirm operations accept stored identity only, recheck current Actor/scope/permission/expiry and exact locked snapshot. Business writer, audits/notifications and idempotent receipt share one transaction. Rights are checked again on retry/replay; budget data is masked if revoked.
- Reuse the existing review UI, exact source and before/after facts, early recovery feedback, quiet details at row end and a second protected confirmation. No selection/loading infrastructure duplication or resize remounts.
